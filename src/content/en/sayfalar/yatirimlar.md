## How many of the 3,000+ startups work in sustainability?

There is no official figure. Ankara Kalkınma Ajansı's 3,000+ figure covers all technology startups from 2018 to 2025 and gives no breakdown by sector; startups.watch and Invest in Türkiye reports also have no count of climate or sustainability startups for Ankara. The indicators we have:

- **About 51 startups/companies on the map** work in sustainability: 10 have their own startup entries (Evreka, Inavitas, FarmLabs, MicroFert, TRIPY, Sine Enerji, Biomass Enerji, DOA Smart, CFL, AISField), and the rest appear in technopark company lists (ONO Yazılım, SESASİS, Sputek, 8 energy/agriculture/environment companies at OSTİM Teknopark). This is a lower bound, not a full survey.
- **About 9% of startups that raised investment:** 4 of the 46 investment deals with a stated sector in 2025 were "Sustainability & Environment". The share by amount is very low: about $650K of $41 million (about 1.6%).
- **Compared with Türkiye as a whole:** startups.watch counted 18 deals tagged "Energy" and 10 tagged "Sustainability" nationwide in 2025 (tags may overlap).

If we apply the investment share (4/59 ≈ 7% to 4/46 ≈ 9%) to all startups, **roughly 200–270** of the 3,000+ startups may be related to sustainability. This is an estimate, not a count; startups that raised investment may not fully represent all startups.
