A technology development centre focused on planetary health, sustainability and climate resilience, set up with private capital and KOSGEB support. It prioritises startups in climate change, clean energy, waste management, the circular economy and health innovation, and is also open to software, AI and games projects. Kamu Araştırmaları Vakfı (KAV) is among its institutional stakeholders.
